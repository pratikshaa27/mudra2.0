# Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) Portal & MUDRA Ltd. Refinance Application

A modern, high-performance web application for **Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency Ltd. (MUDRA)** under the **Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India**.

---

## 🚀 Key Portal Features & Dedicated Pages

- **Home Portal (`/Home`)**: Government of India header, hero slider, live stats counter (₹27.5+ Lakh Cr disbursed), scheme calculator, and chatbot.
- **Offerings (`/Offerings`)**: Detailed breakdown for **Shishu** (Up to ₹50,000), **Kishore** (₹50,000 - ₹5 Lakh), **Tarun** (₹5 Lakh - ₹10 Lakh), and **TarunPlus** (₹10 Lakh - ₹20 Lakh).
- **Financials (`/Financials`)**: Chronologically sorted Annual Reports, Public Disclosures (FY 2025-26 Q1 & Q2), and Form MGT-7 filings with PDF viewers.
- **Gallery (`/Gallery`)**: 5 official categories (`MUDRA INAUGURATION`, `AWARDS`, `CONFERENCES`, `PROGRAMMES`, `10TH FOUNDATION DAY`) featuring PM Modi launch photos.
- **Success Stories (`/SuccessStories`)**: Beneficiary profiles categorized into *Women Entrepreneurs*, *Youth MSME Achievers*, and *Rural Enterprise Success*.
- **Careers (`/Careers`)**: Official MUDRA recruitment notifications, year-wise advertisements (2016-2025), application formats, and caution advisory.
- **FAQ (`/FAQ`)**: Interactive search filter & accordion categories.
- **Contact (`/Contact`)**: Corporate address, toll-free helpline, official PDF directories, and inquiry form.
